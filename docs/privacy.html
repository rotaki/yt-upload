Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

yt-upload is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It requests a single permission, youtube.upload, which is used only to upload video files the owner chooses, with the title, description, tags and privacy setting the owner provides.
  • It does not read, collect, store, or share any other Google account data.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own machines and environments and is never sent anywhere except Google's APIs.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party services are used.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue.
